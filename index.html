The Institute

About Velum

Velum Institute is an independent research body devoted to the serious study of Christian theology in dialogue with philosophy, history, and the broader intellectual tradition it inherited from antiquity.

Our work proceeds from the conviction that doctrine has a history, that reason and revelation are not adversaries, and that the questions raised by the earliest councils remain unfinished business for contemporary thought.

Who we are

A small, independent fellowship of researchers working across theology, philosophy, and Church history, unaffiliated with any single ecclesial body.

What we research

Doctrine, tradition, scripture, ethics, and the historical development of Christian thought from the apostolic era to the present.

Our method

Close historical reading paired with philosophical analysis, published as papers, essays, and working documents open to scrutiny and revision.
